System pipeline

How it works

One photo goes through up to six stages. Every stage runs locally on a CPU — no internet connection or cloud service is needed.

  1. 1

    Quality gate

    Checks resolution, sharpness, brightness, contrast and leaf coverage. Unusable photos are rejected with retake tips.

  2. 2

    Leaf model

    A CNN classifier over every crop condition plus two router classes. It names the crop and the primary finding — or routes the photo as “not a plant” (rejected) or “insect”.

    Obj 1 · CropObj 2.1 · DiseaseLearned

  3. 3a

    Insect model

    Insect photos go to a second CNN trained on IP102 (102 pest species) for the species, its host crops, damage and control. If it is not trained yet, the photo falls back to leaf analysis with a warning.

    Obj 3.1 · PestsLearned

  4. 3b

    Leaf analysis

    Leaf photos get Grad-CAM plus leaf segmentation and colour/shape measurements: yellowing, necrosis, purpling, marginal scorch, holes and stippling.

    Obj 2.2 · NutrientsObj 3.1 · Pest damage

  5. 4

    Advisory engine

    Combines the model output with the measurements into a transparent health score, then looks up fertilizer, treatment and safety advice in a knowledge base for Indian smallholder farms.

    Health scoreObj 3.2 · Fertilizer

  6. 5

    Report

    A dashboard that says where each finding came from, with explanations, safety notes and a printable report.

Learned or rule-based? Each finding says which.

FindingSourceDetails
Crop and primary diagnosisLearnedLeaf model trained on labelled lab and field photos of every supported crop.
DiseaseLearnedDisease classes of the leaf model; the affected leaf area is measured by image analysis.
Pest damageLearned RulesLearned pest-damage classes where training data exists (e.g. armyworm, jassids, mites, leaf miner); otherwise inferred from virus–vector links or read from holes and stippling.
Nutrient deficiencyLearned RulesLearned for the crops with labelled deficiency photos (banana, citrus, coffee, date palm, groundnut, chilli, lemon, cotton); a leaf-colour rule estimate (N, P, K, Mg, Fe) for the rest.
Insect speciesLearnedInsect model trained on IP102, used only when the leaf model routes the photo as an insect.
Health score, fertilizer and treatmentRulesTransparent penalty model and an agronomic knowledge base; doses are general guidance.

Runs fully offline

The models, image analysis and advisory knowledge base are all bundled with the app. Inference takes well under a second on an ordinary laptop CPU, which makes it suitable for on-device use in the field.

Honest about its limits

Low-confidence and unfamiliar-looking photos are flagged instead of answered silently, and rule-based findings are labelled as estimates. All findings are decision support — confirm with a soil test or your local KVK.
